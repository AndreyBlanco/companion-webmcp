# Synthetic Data Policy

## Rule

Companion WebMCP uses exclusively synthetic data created for the public project.

Synthetic means invented independently for testing or demonstration. It does not mean redacted, pseudonymized, translated, paraphrased or statistically transformed from a real person, case, production log or private document.

## Permitted

- fictional subjects with neutral identifiers;
- invented captures and observations;
- generated edge cases reviewed to avoid resemblance to private records;
- deterministic fixtures created in this repository; and
- publicly documented protocol examples that are license-compatible and contain no personal data.

## Prohibited

- real or deidentified personal information;
- student, family, patient, employee or customer records;
- production prompts, completions, logs, screenshots or exports;
- copied examples from a private host;
- secrets, access tokens or private endpoints;
- datasets whose origin or license cannot be demonstrated; and
- fixtures containing hidden mappings to real identities.

## Fixture record

Every fixture set must identify:

- creator or generator;
- creation date;
- purpose and tests served;
- confirmation that no real or private source was used;
- license or repository ownership; and
- reviewer before publication.

## Incident rule

If private or personal material is suspected, stop work, prevent further distribution, preserve only the minimum evidence required for response, notify the project authority and follow an approved removal and history-rewrite procedure. Do not silently delete and continue.
